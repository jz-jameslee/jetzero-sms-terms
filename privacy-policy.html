Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026 — applies to JetZero Inc.'s internal IT alert SMS notification system

This Privacy Policy describes how JetZero Inc. ("JetZero," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information in connection with our internal SMS alert notification system, used to notify designated IT staff of critical information technology incidents.

Who this applies to

This system is used exclusively by JetZero employees who are IT support technicians or on-call personnel. It is an internal operational tool and is not offered to, or used by, members of the public or customers.

Information we collect

  • Employee name
  • Mobile phone number, provided by the employee for the purpose of receiving critical IT ticket alerts
  • Date of consent to receive these alerts

How we use this information

We use this information solely to send automated SMS alerts when a critical-priority ticket is created or updated in JetZero's internal IT service management system. Messages include a ticket reference number, priority level, and a brief description of the issue, so that the recipient can respond promptly.

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Data retention and security

Phone numbers and consent records are maintained internally by JetZero IT management and are retained only for as long as the employee holds a role requiring these alerts. Access to this information is limited to authorized IT administrators.

Your choices

Employees may withdraw consent to receive these alerts at any time by notifying JetZero IT management. Upon withdrawal, the employee's phone number will be removed from the alert system.

Contact us

Questions about this Privacy Policy may be directed to JetZero Inc.'s IT department through standard internal channels.
